# Returns and Refunds Handbook

<!-- access: agent -->
## Return windows

- Standard items: returnable within **60 days** of delivery.
- Sale items: returnable within **30 days** of delivery.

The window starts on the delivery date in the order history.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5 to 7 business days** after the returned item is received at the warehouse.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Agent refund authority

Agents can approve refunds up to **$150** per order without lead approval. Anything above $150 goes to a lead (see the Escalation Matrix).

<!-- access: lead -->
## Lead refund authority

Leads can approve refunds from **$150.01 to $1,000** per order. Refunds above $1,000 require approval from the Finance manager.

Leads can issue goodwill credit of up to **$50** per customer per order at their discretion. Log every goodwill credit in the order notes.
