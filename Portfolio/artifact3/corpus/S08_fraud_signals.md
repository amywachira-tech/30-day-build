# Fraud and Serial-Returner Signals (Leads only)

<!-- access: lead -->
## Flagging criteria

Flag an account if any of the following apply:

- **4 or more returns in 90 days**
- Return value above **60% of total spend in the last 12 months**
- **2 or more "item not received" claims in 6 months**

<!-- access: lead -->
## Handling flagged accounts

All refunds on a flagged account need lead approval, whatever the amount.

**Never tell the customer that the account is flagged** or describe these criteria to them. Explain any delay as a standard review.
