# Identity Verification FAQ

Policy dated March 2024.

<!-- access: agent -->
## How do I verify a caller?

Ask for the customer's full name and the order number. If both match the order, the caller is verified.

<!-- access: agent -->
## What can I share once the caller is verified?

You can share the order status, tracking information and the delivery address on the order.
