# Warranty Claims FAQ

<!-- access: agent -->
## Warranty periods

- Furniture: **2 years** on structural defects (frames, joints, mechanisms).
- Appliances: **1 year**.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Making a claim

The customer needs proof of purchase. Claims are submitted through the warranty form on the website. Normal wear and tear is not covered.
