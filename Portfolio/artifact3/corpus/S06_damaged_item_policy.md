# Damaged Item Policy (Kestrel-sold items)

This policy covers items sold and shipped by Kestrel. For items sold by marketplace sellers, use the Marketplace Seller Returns Policy.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Reporting window

The customer must report damage within **14 days** of delivery and send at least one photo of the damage.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Resolution

Kestrel refunds or replaces the item straight away, at the customer's choice. For items under **$75**, the customer does not need to return the damaged item.

Damaged items valued over $500 go to a lead before resolution (see the Escalation Matrix).
