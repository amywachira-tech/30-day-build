# Escalation Matrix

<!-- access: agent -->
## When to escalate to a lead

| Situation | Escalate to |
|---|---|
| Refund above $150 | Lead |
| Account shows a fraud flag | Lead |
| Data deletion or privacy request | Lead, who forwards to Legal |
| Damaged item valued over $500 | Lead |
| Customer asks for a manager | Lead |

<!-- access: agent -->
## How to escalate

Assign the ticket to the "Leads" group in Zendesk with a one-line summary. On a live call, tell the customer a lead will follow up within one business day.
