# Payment Dispute Playbook (Leads)

<!-- access: lead -->
## Duplicate charges

This includes duplicate charges through Klarna. The lead confirms the duplicate in the payment dashboard and refunds it within **24 hours**.

<!-- access: lead -->
## Chargebacks

Respond to every chargeback notice within **7 days**. Attach the order record and delivery proof.

**Never admit liability in writing** in a chargeback response or in any message to the customer about a dispute.
