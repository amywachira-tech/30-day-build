# Customer Identity Verification

<!-- access: agent -->
## Required checks

Before discussing any order, verify the caller with all of the following:

1. Full name
2. The email address on the account
3. One of: the last 4 digits of the payment card, or the billing postcode

An order number on its own is **never** enough to verify a caller.

<!-- access: agent -->
## What you can share

Once verified, you can share order status and tracking. **Never read out the full delivery address.** You may confirm the city only.

<!-- access: agent -->
## If verification fails

Do not share any order details. Offer to send the information to the email address on file.
