# Refund Approval Guide (Leads)

<!-- access: lead -->
## Approval limits

- Leads approve refunds from **$150.01 to $1,000** per order.
- Refunds above **$1,000** need the Finance manager's approval. Send the order number and reason by email and wait for written approval before processing.

<!-- access: lead -->
## Before approving

Check the account for a fraud flag before approving any refund. If the account is flagged, follow the Fraud and Serial-Returner Signals page.
