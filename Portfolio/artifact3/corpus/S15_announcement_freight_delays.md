# #policy-updates

<!-- access: agent -->
**Posted by Operations, Sep 29, 2026**

Temporary change: freight delays

Our freight carrier is short on capacity. From **October 1 to November 15, 2026**, freight shipments (large furniture) are considered lost after **15 business days** with no tracking movement, instead of 10.

Standard parcels are not affected and stay at 10 business days.

We will post again when this ends.
