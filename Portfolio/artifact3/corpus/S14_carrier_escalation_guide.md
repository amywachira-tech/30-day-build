# Carrier Escalation Guide

<!-- access: agent -->
## Opening a carrier trace

If tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, open a trace with the carrier from the carrier portal. Tell the customer the trace is open and that you will update them within 2 business days.

<!-- access: lead -->
## Early reship authority

Leads may authorize a reship before the shipment is confirmed lost, for orders under **$300**, when a trace is open and the customer needs the item urgently. Note the reason in the order.
