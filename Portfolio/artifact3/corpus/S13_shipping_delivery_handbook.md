# Shipping and Delivery Handbook

<!-- access: agent -->
## Delivery times

- Standard parcels: **3 to 5 business days**.
- Freight (large furniture): **7 to 14 business days**. The carrier calls the customer to book a delivery slot.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Lost shipments

A shipment is considered lost after **10 business days** with no movement on the carrier's tracking scans.

Once a shipment is lost, offer the customer a reship or a full refund.
