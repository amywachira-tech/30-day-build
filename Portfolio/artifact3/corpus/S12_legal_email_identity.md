# Email from Legal

<!-- access: agent -->
**From:** Grace, Legal
**To:** Customer Support (all agents and leads)
**Date:** July 20, 2026
**Subject:** Identity verification requirements, effective July 21

Effective July 21, 2026, every caller must be verified with their full name, the email address on the account, and either the last 4 digits of the payment card or the billing postcode before any order is discussed. An order number alone does not verify a caller.

Do not read out a full delivery address under any circumstances. You may confirm the city.

If the caller cannot give the email address on the account, do not give tracking information by phone. Send the tracking link to the email address on the account instead.

These requirements replace all earlier guidance on caller verification.
