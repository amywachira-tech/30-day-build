# #policy-updates

<!-- access: agent -->
**Posted by Finance Operations, Jun 10, 2026**

Policy change: Final Clearance items

Effective Tuesday, June 16, 2026, items tagged **"Final Clearance"** on the product page and receipt are **final sale**. They cannot be returned or refunded, except where they arrive damaged (follow the damaged item process).

All other sale items stay at the **30-day** return window from delivery. Standard items stay at 60 days.

Check the receipt for the "Final Clearance" tag before approving any sale item return.
