# Marketplace Seller Returns Policy

This policy covers items sold by third-party marketplace sellers on the Kestrel website. The seller name appears on the product page and the receipt as "Sold by [seller]". For items sold by Kestrel, use the Damaged Item Policy.

<!-- access: agent -->
## Damaged marketplace items

The customer must report damage within **7 days** of delivery and include photos.

The agent opens a claim with the seller through **Seller Hub**. The seller is responsible for the refund or replacement.

<!-- access: agent -->
## When Kestrel steps in

If the seller has not responded to the claim within **3 business days**, Kestrel refunds the customer and recovers the cost from the seller.
